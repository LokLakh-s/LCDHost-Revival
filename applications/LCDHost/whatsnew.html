Whats new in LCDHost 0.3.0:

  • G-keys, M-keys and LCD buttons of the G13, G15, G19, G510 and Z-10 can be used in layouts
  • Backlight: the right settings for each keyboard (colour or brightness level)
  • Linux: an AppImage, and a button to install the udev rules from the plugin settings
  • The Logitech plugins tell in their settings whether LCDHost can use the device, and why not
  • The plugin for Logitech Gaming Software is no longer included

Always back-up your layouts before updating.
